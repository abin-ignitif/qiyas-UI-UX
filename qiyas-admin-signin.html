Admin authentication · Qiyas

A provisioned administrator signs in without ceremony.

One quiet unauthenticated surface for the single admin role at launch. Access is provisioned by the Qiyas operator, role is resolved after authentication, and the screen never offers self-signup, social login, marketing, a persistence option, or an invented second factor.

Recommended Applied

One unauthenticated shell: a brand rail beside a 480px Ink-led form column on desktop, a single column on mobile, and an in-product locale switch at the logical top end. Sessions are not kept by default, so the status line says so plainly.

Rejected Not built

Signup, social login, trust seals, gradient or celebration states, a “keep me signed in” checkbox, biometric unlock, a role switch, and any second-factor surface. Gold is absent; green stays reserved for correctness and focus.

One surface, both canvases.

The mobile and desktop canvases are independent clones of one template, so neither resets the other. Locale, theme and the demo state below stay in sync across both; typed values survive every failure state and are never written to localStorage.

Gap list

Open decisions stay visible.

Per the gap-list convention, nothing here was invented to make the screen look finished.

  • Admin second factor / SSO

    Undefined in the system. Do not invent an authenticator, recovery-code, or identity-provider surface; only email and password are shown.

  • Admin account provisioning / activation

    How the first admin account is created, invited, and activated has no designed surface. This is an operations gap, not a screen.

  • Admin session lifetime and device policy

    Session length, idle lock, and device trust are unspecified. The status line states only that sessions are not kept by default.

  • Admin role resolution

    Super admin versus organisation admin is resolved after authentication and never chosen on this screen; the resolution rules and default landing surface are undefined.

  • Audit logging requirements

    What sign-in events are logged, retained, and exposed to the operator is not defined.

  • Password policy source of truth

    The minimum password rules and their enforcement point are not confirmed; sign-in performs no client-side policy beyond presence.

  • Email delivery and resend contract

    Delivery provider, resend cooldown, and anti-abuse thresholds for the recovery flow are unspecified.

Guardrails

Do and Don't.

The auth surface stays quiet, truthful, and provisioned-only.

Do
  • Keep exactly one Ink primary action per surface.
  • Preserve typed values in every failure state and focus the first invalid field.
  • Keep credentials LTR-isolated with Western digits and tabular figures.
  • State plainly that admin sessions are not kept by default.
Don't
  • Add signup, marketing, trust seals, or social login.
  • Add a “keep me signed in” checkbox or biometric unlock.
  • Invent second-factor or SSO flows.
  • Use gold, gradients, or celebration states.
