Screen 17 · Student progress · learner-web

The same evidence, without a chart dependency.

This demo-safe revision follows the delivered 14 October P0 scope and the 07 November demo plan. It is built on practice sets, practice assessments, scheduled exams, automatic evaluation, and results/review. No chart library, no SVG plotting, no axis maths.

Resolution

Evidence first, shipped simply. A range-selectable week scorecard replaces the momentum chart (2, 3, or 6 weeks), followed by four period totals, one evidence-led focus recommendation, topic mastery by difficulty level, and the assessment record. Every surface is HTML and CSS; the recommendation gives the screen a clear next step.

Removed to avoid dashboard overlap

Continue-practice hero, at-a-glance stat strip, this-week panel, upcoming-exam list, and current-level panel belong to the dashboard, scheduled exams, and learning library. The chart and sparkline are removed for demo delivery; the week range stays as a simple 2, 3, or 6-week selector. Badge exams, ladder, streaks, and pass/fail badges remain out of scope.

Live preview

One screen, two navigation shapes.

Theme, locale, and data state drive both canvases through one render function. The desktop canvas uses the student shell with a collapsible rail under the app bar; the mobile canvas keeps the three-destination footer nav. The week scorecard is a range-selectable strip of text tiles, so the weekly story survives without a plotting layer.

Gap list

Open decisions stay visible.

Not hidden inside custom components. Each gap names the missing contract, not a styling preference.

  • Student progress is a V1.2 featureThe delivered 14 October scope provides practice, practice assessment, scheduled exam, evaluation, and result/review data. Student progress (ID 28) and practice/assessment history (ID 29) remain V1.2; this screen is the forward design that consumes the completed data.
  • Admin progress is separateThe 07 November demo includes a Student Progress (Admin) monitoring page with list/filters and attempt/score visibility. This screen is the student's own view; it is not the admin roster. Do not expose scores to parents.
  • Scorecard contractDefine weekly bucketing (Monday–Sunday, Asia/Riyadh), how inactive weeks render, weighted vs unweighted accuracy, study-time aggregation, and the coverage denominator. The displayed tiles and the four totals must use the same server window.
  • Week range selectorThe scorecard offers 2, 3, and 6-week ranges. Confirm whether the choice persists across visits, how the coverage denominator is scoped to the range, and that changing the range never mixes totals from different windows.
  • Focus recommendationDefine how the next topic is selected and prioritized with at least five answers, the tie-breaker, and the fallback when no topic qualifies. The screen must not invent a recommendation.
  • Mastery targetThe 70% line is a demo target, not a pass mark. Confirm whether the target is global or configured per difficulty level before implementation.
  • Strength thresholdsWorking well and needs work are computed from topics with at least five answers, using 80% and 65% cutoffs here. Product must confirm the minimum sample and cutoffs, and the UI must stay silent until the threshold is met.
  • Assessment recordScores come from submitted practice assessments and scheduled exams. Confirm scores use comparable denominators, and define what happens when an assessment is retaken or voided.
  • Cross-screen ownershipThe dashboard owns continue practice, upcoming exams, and current level; this screen owns the scorecard, mastery, and the record. Navigation and deep-link contracts between the two screens remain open.
  • No chart dependencyThe chart, axis, and sparkline are removed for demo delivery. The lightweight week range selector is not a chart. If a trend chart returns later, it needs its own data contract and should not block the scorecard.
  • Removed badge layerBadge exams, the badge ladder, eligibility, cooldowns, and account caps are not part of the current product. Badge configuration and assessment-to-badge remain V1.2 and are not designed here.
  • Arabic native reviewEvery string in this screen needs native review, especially the practice, assessment, mastery, and strength vocabulary.
