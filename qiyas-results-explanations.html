Screen 10 · Student results

The verdict first. The learning after.

This screen answers whether the student passed before asking them to inspect anything else. Explanations are triaged by learning value, and every named weakness routes directly into open material or practice.

Resolution

Make the verdict the first visual block. Put wrong answers first, then correct-but-slow answers, and keep the full question list available only when a student searches or asks for it.

Rejected

A flat twenty-question transcript. It treats every review item as equally urgent and hides the two routes that matter most: the next action and the weakest concept.

Interactive handoff

One result surface, five honest endings.

Document controls preview the four eligibility outcomes and the lighter practice ending in both canvases. Arabic is primary; the product surface switches direction without resetting the selected result or triage tab.

Explanation triage

Review the signal, not the transcript.

Wrong answers appear first because they have immediate learning value. Correct-but-slow answers follow because time is a useful signal without becoming a judgment. The full list stays available through search.

Three review layers

1
Wrong answersShown first; name the concept and route to practice.
2
Correct, slowShown on request; the 90-second signal is useful but not alarming.
3
All questionsAvailable through search when a student wants the complete record.

Rejected: every question in order

A flat transcript makes a 20-question test look like 20 equal tasks. It forces students who missed one concept to hunt and makes the review surface feel longer than the test.

Chosen order

Wrong → correct but slow → searched question. The student can still reach everything, but the surface starts with the highest-value work.

Failure and recovery

Every interruption says what happened next.

These states are shown as persistent inline conditions, not unexplained green or red boxes. The result is private to the student; repeated eligibility failures do not escalate in tone.

Auto-submitted on timeout

Time ended. Your saved answers were submitted automatically. Unanswered questions remain visible in review.

Submitted with unanswered questions

3 questions were unanswered. They are included in review and are not silently removed from the result.

Explanation reported

Your report was sent. Keep reviewing; the question remains visible while the review is pending.

Missing or wrong-language explanation

This explanation is unavailable in العربية right now. The issue is recorded; the question itself remains readable.

Connection lost before result

Your submission is safe. Qiyas is still resolving the result; keep this page open or return when connected.

Repeated failure

This is the same neutral checkpoint state as the first attempt. Practice or learning remains open; there is no escalating warning.

Passed narrowly

Passed. The narrow margin is available in the diagnosis, but the verdict is not softened or reframed as a near miss.

Gap list

Open decisions stay visible.

These are not hidden in the implementation. They need product or platform decisions before the result contract is production-ready.

  • Slow-but-correct surfacingOpen: confirm threshold and whether showing a time signal makes students self-conscious.
  • Reported-question scoreOpen: decide whether a reported question is unchanged, provisionally voided, or resolved later.
  • Attempt historyOpen: decide whether students can revisit past eligibility attempts or only the latest result.
  • Question-pool provenanceOpen: decide whether students see where a question came from or only the authored concept and level.
  • Result delivery contractOpen: define how a safe submission resolves after connection loss and how long the pending state can remain.
  • Missing translation fallbackOpen: define whether the student can switch to the available locale for one explanation or must wait for translation.
  • Report workflowOpen: define form fields, confirmation copy, and status resolution for a reported question.
