Qiyas / قياس · Foundation 01

A precise system for difficult mathematics.

Qiyas is a bilingual competitive-exam preparation app for students aged 14–18. The system is RTL-first, quiet under pressure, and derived from one modular mark: square structure, one diagonal, two dots, one triangle.

View the hero componentsJump to tokens
System thesisControlled pressure

Clarity makes hard work handleable. No mascots, sparkle, confetti, pills, gradients, or cheerful exclamation marks.

Locked choices

Arabic first · maths only · 0–9 numerals · open learning · timed exams

01 / Source

Read the mark first.

The logo is not a mood board. It is a construction system. Every interface rule below is traceable to a visible property of the English and Arabic lockups.

Visual grammar

  • Modular: glyphs are rectangles on an implied square grid; terminals are square.
  • 0.18 stroke ratio: uniform weight becomes the four border weights.
  • One diagonal: the ق tail is the only 45° movement; use it once per screen at most.
  • Three accents: two square dots and one triangle are the complete accent vocabulary.
  • Colour discipline: green carries the mark; gold is scarce; ink is structure; paper is ground.
  • Bidirectional: the mark leads in both lockups, so product chrome mirrors with the locale.
Lockups · traced directly from Sketch
Derivation summary

Orthogonal structure gives the system its quietness; the single diagonal gives progress a rare, legible moment; square accents keep achievement specific rather than decorative.

05 / Motifs

Four shapes. No fifth.

These are not generic decorations. They are four recognizable pieces taken from the Qiyas logo and reused sparingly. Functional icons use Lucide; these shapes carry the brand’s own meaning.

Square frame

Achievement container: badge tiles, level markers, avatar frames.

Actual Qiyas green mark

Green mark + tail

The angled segment is the tail at the end of the green logo mark. It is not a free-floating slash. Use the complete mark, or let this edge lead a progress fill that belongs to a square badge.

Two gold squares · dot pair

These are the two small square accents in the logo. Use them to count or mark a pair of steps, levels, or breadcrumb points. They are not random bullets.

Triangle

Advancement: next action, level-up, upward trend.

Read the logo

Tail = the angled end of the green mark. Dot pair = the two tiny gold squares that mark or enumerate.

Use them in the product

The tail is reserved for the complete logo or a progress edge that clearly belongs to a square badge. A dot pair can show “step 2 of 4” or identify two related levels. Never draw the tail by itself.

Hard rule

No fifth decorative shape may be invented. No rounded illustration language, no sparkle, no glow, no confetti.

09.6 / Extension · Motion

Motion stops hard.

The logo has no curves, so motion has none either. This layer adds orthogonal, decisive movement for comprehension and five signature moment families only. Existing foundation styling remains unchanged.

Motion tokens

TokenDurationUse
instant0msCheckbox, radio, segmented code fill.
quick120msHover, focus, small state change, button press.
base180msMost transitions, inline error, expand/collapse.
deliberate280msSheets, modals, route changes, drawers.
signature600–1000msThe five signature families only. No other surface may use this tier.
Only three easings: enter cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1), exit cubic-bezier(.5,0,.8,.3), linear for measured progress. Stagger is 40ms; tight intra-mark stagger is 25ms.all screens; token consumers in React and Flutter

Motion laws

  • Animate transform and opacity only. Width, height, and physical offsets change only through FLIP.
  • Forward motion travels toward logical end; Arabic reverses the physical travel direction.
  • 60fps on a mid-range Android is the floor.
  • Reduced motion keeps the information and drops the travel: arrive at the end state, never disappear without feedback.
  • One bold moment at a time. No spring, elastic, bounce, overshoot, or decorative loop.
every animated surface, including splash and award moments

Never animate

The exam timer changes digits only. Wrong-answer feedback never shakes, buzzes, flashes, or sounds. Loading skeletons are static tonal blocks with no shimmer sweep. On a badge exam, nothing moves unless it is the student's own action. Timers communicate urgency through colour and weight, not motion.

Mathematics in RTL

  • Inline equations stay LTR, sit on the text baseline, and get 4px inline padding.
  • Display equations are centred within the content column, not mirrored to read backwards.
  • Multi-line working, fractions, radicals, matrices, coordinate axes, number lines, inequality signs, and integrals remain LTR.

Implementation rules
