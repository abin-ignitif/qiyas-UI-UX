Gap list

Open decisions stay visible.

Not hidden in custom components. Each gap names what is missing and why the screen stayed conservative.

  • Result contractThe pending screen names the exam and the release time only; the result endpoint and its field names belong to the published result screen.
  • Release deliveryWhether the result becomes available by polling, push, or a background refresh is a platform decision; this screen recomputes the countdown from the release time.
  • Auto hand-offWhen the countdown reaches zero the prototype stops at 00:00:00; production routes to qiyas-result-published.html at that moment.
  • Countdown maximumHours beyond two digits (for multi-day release windows) are a product decision; the prototype pads to two digits.
  • Arabic calendar and digitsar-SA defaults to Hijri; the prototype pins Gregorian with Western digits to match the system numeral rule.
