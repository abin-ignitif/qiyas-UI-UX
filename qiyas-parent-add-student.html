Canonical sequence

Five pages, two access variants.

Page 03 is the only decision point: email is direct delivery; no email is parent-mediated handoff. The explanation is equal in weight so no-email never reads as second best.

States and recovery

Interruptions do not erase the flow.

Each failure names what happened and the smallest action that gets the parent back to a safe state. Duplicate creation and reshare stay visibly different.

Gap list

  • Grade changes and progressCan the grade change later, and what happens to already-started material and cleared levels?
  • Invite revocationCan a parent invalidate a link sent to the wrong person, and how quickly does it stop working?
  • Resend policyHow many resends are allowed, and does resending reuse or rotate the link and password?
  • Two parents, one childWhat does the second parent see, and how is household ownership joined without a duplicate?
  • First-login notificationIs the parent notified when the child first logs in, and through what channel?
  • No-email credential contractDefine credential lifetime, one-time use, server-side first-use state, rotation, audit trail, and parent retrieval limits.
  • Duplicate matchingDefine the matching key, collision handling, and privacy-safe message when two parents try to create the same child.

ما ليس هذا المسار؟What is not this flow

إعادة إرسال دعوة معلّقة لا تنشئ طالبًا. إنها عملية مجاورة، باسم مختلف، وتبدأ من لوحة ولي الأمر أو حالة الانتظار.Resending a pending invite does not create a student. It is an adjacent flow with a different name, reached from the parent dashboard or waiting state.

المسار يعرف مصدر دخوله حتى يعود إلى لوحة ولي الأمر الصحيحة: بعد التسجيل، من اللوحة، أو من مبدّل الأطفال.The flow remembers its origin so it returns to the right parent context: after sign-up, from dashboard, or from the child switcher.
