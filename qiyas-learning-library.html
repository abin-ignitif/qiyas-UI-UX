Screen 08 · authenticated student surface

Everything is open. One next step stays obvious.

Concepts form the stable browsing spine. Level and badge requirements are lenses over the same cards, so curiosity never meets a lock and directed study never loses its map.

Recommended

Concept spine · level lens

Students browse Algebra → Quadratic equations. A level filter shows the rigour used by eligibility and badges without duplicating the learning card.

Rejected

Level-only tree

It repeats the same concept at several difficulties and makes students wonder whether they already read it. A concept-only tree was also rejected because it cannot answer badge requirements.

One-step proof

Three questions, one route each.

The organising decision is only useful if the student can act without translating between “levels” and “concepts.”

Progress treatment rejected

Bars, percentages, and “0 of N” on every concept turn open browsing into a backlog. The shipped treatment marks only cleared or in-progress items; untouched material is visually neutral.

State coverage

Freedom remains intact when data does not.

The state selector demonstrates new, partially worked, deep-linked, loading, offline, failed-card, and missing-locale behavior without replacing the whole library with an error screen.

Brand new

One recommended first concept leads. Untouched cards carry no debt labels, unread counts, or completion bars.

Partially worked

Only touched items show a quiet square marker and “Cleared” or “In progress.”

Deep-linked arrival

The requested card opens immediately. Breadcrumbs and “Browse Algebra” provide a one-step way out.

Loading, offline, and failed load

Loading keeps structure visible. Previously opened text remains readable under the explicit cache assumption. One failed card does not disable the surrounding library.

Missing translation

The Arabic route names the missing version and shows the English source rather than a blank card.

Reading ahead

Advanced concepts look exactly like nearby concepts. No warning, lock, or “are you sure” interruption is introduced.

Flutter handoff

Mobile geometry and interaction contract.

Use SafeArea, one 16px logical inset, normal vertical flow, and a scroll view when text scaling or a short viewport exceeds the canvas. Do not shrink type or controls.

390 / 360

390 − 16 − 16 = 358px usable content. At 360px the content measure is 328px. No document gutter is added inside the product surface.

Targets and flow

All controls are at least 44px. Practice and eligibility actions remain distinct at 200% text scale. Product bottom navigation stays outside the reading card content flow.

SafeArea and scroll

Top bar respects the top SafeArea; bottom navigation includes the gesture inset. The active card scrolls normally and equations never force horizontal page scrolling.

Direction and math

Use logical padding and ordering. Equations use explicit LTR isolation inside Arabic copy; Western Arabic digits remain unchanged.

Gap list

Open decisions remain explicit.

The prototype stays conservative where product, content, backend, or platform behavior is not settled.

  • TODO · Commercial cap boundaryConfirm that material and practice remain open above the account cap and only exams are capped. The screen assumes this reading but does not enforce a paywall.
  • TODO · SearchDefine the content-volume threshold, ranking, Arabic stemming, typo tolerance, and whether search spans concepts, card text, or both. No one-off search field is invented here.
  • TODO · Eligibility prerequisiteDecide whether the test is always open, requires a short practice check, or uses another rule. The UI only makes “timed and counts” unmistakable and routes through test details.

  • TODO · Saved cardsConfirm whether “Save” syncs across devices, belongs in Profile, or remains device-local. The prototype exposes the action because finding a useful card again is required.
