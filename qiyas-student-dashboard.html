Resolution

The climb explains the return. The next thing earns the tap.

Recommended: keep the ladder visible but subordinate it to one specific, time-bounded action. Reject leading with ten equal badge tiles: it asks a tired student to decide before showing what matters tonight.

Recommended · two-level hierarchy

First: one actionable next thing with topic, difficulty, question count, time, and a single primary action.

Second: the ten-rung ladder with current position and finite progress. It remains visible enough to make the work feel connected to a larger climb.

Rejected · trophy-first dashboard

Making the ladder the hero creates a trophy case. The nightly student must scan ten badges to discover what to do, so “open the app” becomes a decision burden.

Also rejected: a generic “Continue learning” CTA. It is too vague to be actionable.

The ceiling

Show the height without making it a verdict.

All ten badges remain present from day one. The solution is not to hide the ceiling; it is to make distance legible without turning locked into forbidden.

Treatment A · full ladder, calm lock

Show all ten with compact square frames, honest locked labels, and no repeated “not available” alarm. The first available rung gets the strongest outline; the upper rungs recede tonally.

Strength: honest ambition and a clear finite road.

Treatment B · focus window

Show the current rung, two ahead, and a quiet “7 more levels” summary. Expand the full ladder on demand.

Strength: lower daily cognitive load. Cost: hides the very ceiling that makes the product distinctive.

Recommendation: Treatment A for the dashboard. Use tone, size, and status language to reduce pressure; do not hide the product’s promise behind a disclosure.

State coverage

One shell, eleven honest states.

The state toggle in the document header previews the same dashboard with different next things, badge states, and recovery messages. Loading, empty, error, and offline are included because a dashboard cannot be blank when the network is not ready.

Brand new

First step is small and specific. Badge 1 is next-up; the other nine are locked but still visible.

Working toward

Daily eligibility or learning work leads. The ladder says Badge 1 is earned and Badge 2 is next.

Ready to attempt

The exam is available. The primary action changes to the badge exam and the eligible tile becomes the sharpest state.

Cooldown

Targeted practice leads; the countdown is explanatory, not punitive. Badge exam is unavailable until the date arrives.

Account cap

Use the shared capped badge state. It is distinct from locked and keeps the account limit separate from ability.

Returning after a lapse

No streak, absence counter, or guilt copy. Suggest one refresher tied to the climb.

All badges earned

The ladder becomes quiet permanence. Next thing becomes open advanced practice so the screen does not dead-end.

Loading / empty / error / offline

Skeleton, empty state, error state, and offline banner reuse the canonical data-state components. Cached climb remains visible offline.

After completion

Make the return feel causally connected.

Immediately after a practice set, eligibility test, or badge exam, the dashboard should reflect the changed state without a reload or a celebratory detour.

01 · Save

Keep the result

Update the latest activity, accuracy, and concept coverage first. Never show the old progress as if nothing happened.

02 · Move

Advance the next thing

Replace the completed action with the next required piece. If a badge was earned, the next-up tile and progress segment resolve together.

03 · Explain

Show the delta

Use “Updated just now” and a small factual change note. The student should see that the work counted without confetti or praise language.

Gap list

Open decisions, named rather than hidden.

These are not invented into the screen. They require product, backend, or platform decisions before Flutter implementation.

  • Dashboard data freshness contractDefine which fields are cached, how quickly cooldown eligibility refreshes, and whether “Updated just now” means local save or server confirmation.
  • Completion event payloadBackend must return next thing, ladder state, accuracy, and concept coverage in one consistent response so the dashboard can update without a full reload.
  • Advanced practice after Badge 10The system defines the ten-badge ceiling but not the post-completion practice destination. Confirm whether advanced practice is open, curated, or a separate route.
  • Offline action queueThe visual offline state is defined; the product still needs a policy for queued practice attempts, conflict resolution, and server-time reconciliation.
