Eligibility

Who can register?

Students can register for exam events across different grade levels. Students may participate in exams for their current grade or a higher grade, but not a lower grade.

Student registration

Students

Students can register directly using their details.

Guardian registration

Parents & Guardians

Parents or guardians can register on behalf of a student by providing the student’s details.

Registration process

How to Register

Register for an exam event in a few simple steps.

  1. 01

    Choose registration type

    Register as a student or through a parent/guardian.

  2. 02

    Enter student details

    Provide the student's full name, email, school, and current grade.

  3. 03

    Submit registration & pay

    Submit the registration form and complete the event registration payment.

  4. 04

    Receive your exam invitation

    Receive an invitation link to access the exam after the required registration and payment steps.

Registration does not automatically guarantee exam access. Exam access is provided through the invitation link.
